Gmail Email Open Tracker Settings

Extension configuration & instructions
📡 Server Configuration
Enter your public HTTPS URL (e.g. https://your-app.onrender.com) or http://localhost:5000 for local testing.
⚠️ Localhost Warning: If your server URL is set to localhost or 127.0.0.1, emails you send to other people cannot contact your computer, and Google's Image Proxy will reject the connection! For external tracking, you must use a public HTTPS URL (e.g. via ngrok http 5000 or free hosting on Render).
⚙️ Tracking & Transparency
Enable open tracking by default
Automatically inject 1x1 tracking pixel when sending emails from Gmail. You can also toggle tracking per-email directly inside Gmail's compose window.
Visible disclosure footer
Appends a subtle transparency line in the email footer.
📖 How It Works
  1. Open mail.google.com and click Compose.
  2. You'll notice a green 👁️ Tracked badge in the bottom toolbar next to the Send button.
  3. When you click Send (or press Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter), the extension automatically calls /new on your backend server using the subject and recipient as the label.
  4. It inserts the invisible 1x1 tracking pixel and footer disclosure into the email body right before sending.
  5. When the recipient opens the email, your backend server logs the open event! Opens from your filtered IP are automatically excluded from the count.
