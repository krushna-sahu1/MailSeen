    Settings

    How your own opens are hidden

    Opens are counted as yours, not the recipient's, when they come from:

    • a network where you use the extension or this dashboard (learned automatically, kept {{ self_device_days }} days),
    • your own Gmail tab: the extension hides the pixel in your Sent mail,
    • an IP on your "always ignore" list below.

    Missed one? Expand an email and click This was me on the open.
