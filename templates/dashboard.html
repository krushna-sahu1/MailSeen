💡 How Email Tracking Works

1. Why didn't a recipient open show up?

When you send an email with an invisible 1x1 tracking pixel, the recipient's email client loads the image from your server.

If your server URL is http://localhost:5000, other people and Google Image Proxy cannot connect to your computer's localhost. To track emails over the internet, your server must have a public HTTPS URL.

2. Fast Solution: Use a Tunnel

Open a terminal on your computer and run either:

📋 ngrok http 5000 📋 npx localtunnel --port 5000

Copy the HTTPS URL generated (e.g. https://abc.ngrok-free.app), paste it into the Extension Popup under Server URL, and click Save!

3. Permanent Solution: Free Cloud Hosting

Deploy this repository to Render.com, Railway, or Fly.io. You will get a permanent HTTPS link that stays online 24/7 without needing your laptop open.
